These are model estimates, not current conditions. Seasonal curves come from a regression with year as a fixed effect; each source's curve is pulled toward its 50-mile stretch and then the trail-wide curve, so sparse sources don't overfit, and the shaded band is an approximate 80% range. Hover a chart to read the model at any date. Quantity: 0 dry, 1 stagnant/pools, 2 trickle, 3 flowing, 4 abundant. Never rely on this as your only water information.
